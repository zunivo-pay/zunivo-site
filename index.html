For people

The app at app.zunivo.io uses the same contracts.

Payment links

One link, one amount. The payer uses any wallet, or a passkey if they don't have one.

Send

USDC to a .agent name or an address. Lands in about a second, both sides get a receipt.

Scheduled sends

Lock a payment until a date. Once locked it can't be pulled back; after the date anyone can trigger the release, and it can only go to the recipient.

Splits

One payment, split between 2 to 20 addresses in the same transaction. Percentages are fixed when the split is created.

.agent names

An ERC-721 that resolves to a payout address. Whoever holds the name gets the USDC sent to it. The holder can also publish an API endpoint on it, which is how agents find services. 1 USDC to mint.

Questions

Do you hold my money?

No. A payment goes from the payer to the recipient inside one transaction. A scheduled send sits in the ScheduledSends contract, which has no function that lets us move it. There is no balance held on your behalf anywhere.

What does it cost?

Gas on Arc is a fraction of a cent and is paid in USDC. The router takes 0.5% of each payment; the cap in the contract is 1%. A .agent name is 1 USDC.

Is a .agent name a domain?

No. It's an NFT on Arc that resolves to a payout address, and you can transfer or sell it. It has nothing to do with DNS or with Unstoppable Domains' .agent TLD.

Can I try it without real money?

Yes. testnet.zunivo.io runs the same code against Arc testnet with faucet USDC.
