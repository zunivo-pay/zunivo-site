Questions

Do my customers need a crypto wallet?

No. Any EVM wallet works — Zunivo adds the Arc network for them automatically — or they can pay with no wallet at all: a passkey (Face ID / Touch ID) creates a secure Circle smart account on the spot.

What does Zunivo cost?

Free during testnet. At mainnet, a protocol fee of up to 1% on payments — the cap is enforced by the contract, not a promise — and 1 USDC to mint a name.

Why is Zunivo on Arc?

Arc is Circle's chain built for money: USDC is the native gas token and settlement is deterministic and sub-second. It's the first place a stablecoin checkout can feel like a card payment.

Is a .agent name a real domain?

It's an on-chain payment name, not a DNS domain: an ERC-721 on Arc that any app can resolve permissionlessly. Inside Zunivo it works everywhere you'd otherwise paste an address.
